title: Trace who can still use your product during a sign-in outage
description: Move a connected note on a real whiteboard and give support a diagram they can use on their next call.
group: Canvas
demo: outage
---

A customer on the phone can still see their workspace. A new customer has just tried signing in and can’t open theirs. Put both accounts on one whiteboard so support can tell which answer to give.

1. On the diagram, follow the arrows from the new customer to the sign-in problem. Find the separate path for someone with an existing session.
2. Move the support note beside the blocked customer. Watch the connector follow the note. Open the diagram in Slatebox to keep that arrangement.
3. Ask support to edit the note with the actual link they would send. Ask engineering to add a connected note describing what would make the existing session stop working. Each person can challenge the other’s assumptions on the same diagram.
4. Reopen the saved slate at your next review. Read both customer paths aloud to check whether each person would get a useful answer.

The diagram records what your team knows about its product. Your team supplies the dependencies and fallback. Slatebox keeps the connections attached as the drawing changes.
