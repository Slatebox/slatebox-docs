## Publish a canvas walkthrough

1. Open your slate and choose **Present**. As an editor, you get the controls to arrange and publish the viewpoints. [Set the order](present-the-evidence.md)
2. Choose **Publish presentation**. Open **View previous versions** and find the version marked **Published**.
3. Choose **Copy embed code** and paste the iframe into your handbook's HTML or embed block. If the page takes only links, choose **Copy URL**.

The link is unlisted. To list it publicly, turn on **Make presentation public** in **Presentation Settings** before you publish.

## Publish a workshop handoff

A slate with a workshop publishes the same way. Its lanes open the presentation in their set order, each framed with its cards, and the slate's other notes follow. The next shift can open it any time.

Try it with the [theatre cue sheet](https://slatebox.com/use-cases/creative-handoffs/#rehearsal): move **Warm sidelight up** to **Door opens** and select **Open this rehearsal in Slatebox**.

1. On your slate, send `Present Door opens first` in the side conversation and keep the change.
2. Choose **Present** and step through the scenes.
3. Choose **Publish presentation**, then **Copy embed code** for a handbook or **Copy URL** for the standalone player.

To update the briefing, keep editing the slate and publish again. To present live while everyone follows along, use **Presentation options → Present to everyone here** instead. [Present a workshop together](../Using-Slatebox/readme.md#present-a-workshop-together)

## Check it as a reader

1. Open the handbook in a signed-out browser.
2. Start the presentation, focus the player and use the arrow keys to move between viewpoints.
3. To take it down, open **View previous versions** and choose **Unpublish presentation**. Reload the handbook: the player shows **Presentation unavailable**.

For a smaller preview, embed the walkthrough's snapshot image. Readers open the full presentation from it. [Embed a snapshot](../Developers/SlateboxAPI/readme.md#embed-a-published-board)
