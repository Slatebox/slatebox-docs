Advance to **Flood marks** in this exhibition walkthrough. Step back to the **Listening post**, then continue to the **Visitor sketchbook**. A reader can revisit a detail while keeping the handbook open.

## Publish your walkthrough

1. Open your slate. Beside **Present**, open **Presentation options → Edit or publish presentation**. Choose the viewpoints you want readers to visit and save the sequence.
2. Choose **Publish**. Open **View previous versions** and find the version marked **Published**.
3. Choose **Copy embed code**. Paste the iframe into the HTML or embed block in your handbook. For a page that accepts only links, choose **Copy URL** instead.

## Try it as a reader

Open the handbook in a signed-out browser. Start the presentation, focus the player and use the arrow keys to move between viewpoints. To take it down, open **View previous versions** and choose **Unpublish presentation**. Reload the handbook to check that the player shows **Presentation unavailable**.

For a smaller preview, use the published snapshot. Readers can open the full presentation from the image preview. [Embed a snapshot](../Developers/SlateboxAPI/readme.md#embed-a-published-board)
