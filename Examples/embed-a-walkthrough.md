Advance to **Flood marks** in this exhibition walkthrough. Step back to the **Listening post**, then continue to the **Visitor sketchbook**. A reader can revisit a detail while keeping the handbook open.

## Publish canvas viewpoints

1. Open your slate. Beside **Present**, open **Presentation options → Edit or publish presentation**. Choose the viewpoints you want readers to visit and save the sequence.
2. Choose **Publish**. Open **View previous versions** and find the version marked **Published**.
3. Choose **Copy embed code**. Paste the iframe into the HTML or embed block in your handbook. For a page that accepts only links, choose **Copy URL** instead.

## Publish a workshop handoff

Try the [theatre cue sheet](https://slatebox.com/use-cases/creative-handoffs/#rehearsal). Move **Warm sidelight up** to **Door opens** and select **Open this rehearsal in Slatebox**. The evening crew should hear which light comes up as the actor enters.

1. On your private slate, use **Present** to review every scene and its cards. Use **Presentation order** on the board to change the opening scene.
2. Choose **Publish presentation** on the board. Select **Anyone with the link** for an unlisted briefing or **Public** for a public presentation. Select **Publish** to share this version. The audience receives all the scenes and their cards. Your slate keeps its existing sharing settings.
3. Choose **Copy embed code** for a handbook, or copy the viewer link to open the briefing on its own. A reader can advance from **Door opens** to **Actor sits** and step back to the first cue.
4. Change a cue on the working board. Open the viewer link again to see the original briefing. Choose **Republish** when the next crew should receive the change. Its existing viewer link and embed open the new version. To take the briefing down, choose **Withdraw presentation** and reload the viewer.

## Try it as a reader

Open the handbook in a signed-out browser. Start the presentation, focus the player and use the arrow keys to move between viewpoints. For a published canvas walkthrough, open **View previous versions** and choose **Unpublish presentation**. Reload the handbook to check that the player shows **Presentation unavailable**.

For a smaller preview of a published canvas walkthrough, use its snapshot image. Readers can open the full presentation from the image preview. [Embed a snapshot](../Developers/SlateboxAPI/readme.md#embed-a-published-board)
