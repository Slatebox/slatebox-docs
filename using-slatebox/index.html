Slatebox user guide

Read Markdown
View agent prompt
Help me complete my task in Slatebox using the official app guide. Ask which outcome I want, then walk me through the relevant steps and verify it on my slate.

Read https://docs.slatebox.com/Using-Slatebox/readme.md first. Ask for the inputs you need. Tell me what you verified and which steps I still need to do.

If your client cannot fetch the documentation, read the same published file at https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Slatebox/slatebox-docs/retype/Using-Slatebox/readme.md.

Slatebox is free and open source: one live canvas for you, your team and your agents. To have your agent draw for you, start with Use Slatebox from your agent.

Start your first slate

  1. Choose Create slate. Create a slate
  2. Sketch on the canvas, or describe the work in the conversation beside it. “Draw a flow from Request to Review to Approve” gives you three connected, editable notes.
  3. Paste steps or interview notes to give the conversation material. Ask a follow-up such as “Diagram the services in these notes. Show which depend on storage, and leave the reporting job separate.” It uses the notes and connections already on the canvas, and your AI: Pro credits or your balance on Slatebox Cloud, or your own connection on a self-hosted Slatebox.
  4. Review the proposed notes and arrows, then choose Keep changes.

To add a shape, select one and tap a round connection handle: a connected shape appears, ready for its name. Drag the handle into open space to place it. To rename a shape, select it and type, or double-tap it. Try a connector on the sound map

To see a source next to its diagram, open the call-status example and choose Open the call diagram in a private slate. See the call diagram

Name your slate and keep its link. People opening a private link still need permission. If you began as a guest, register in that browser before you rely on the slate from another device.

Use and share from the library

Copy a slate or presentation someone published, add a smart component to your slate, or branch off one and build your own by chatting with Slatebox. Publish yours for everyone after a review. Free. The shared library

Export or import Mermaid

Choose Mermaid in the slate menu to copy or download any slate as Mermaid, or to paste Mermaid in as real notes and lines. It is free and uses no AI. Export and import Mermaid

Keep or discard what agents suggest

When an agent edits your slate, such as Claude through Slatebox's MCP server or a script, its changes appear as ghost notes everyone on the slate sees. Choose Keep or Discard on each, or Keep all on the agent's chip, and undo any agent action. Keep or discard what agents suggest

Invite an outside agent, such as Exa, Linear or GitHub, onto a slate with + Invite agent, and ask it on its chip or by @name in Ask Slatebox. On Slatebox Cloud, with Slatebox Pro or Slatebox Business; on a self-hosted Slatebox, with an on-prem license. Invite an outside agent onto a slate

Add a video

Paste a YouTube, Vimeo or Loom link onto a slate to get a video note with its picture and title. It plays in place when you press play, and inside a presentation. Add a video to a slate

Add a branch to an interactive map

A map has stations, paths between them and a token the team moves. Send each change in the conversation, try it in the preview, and choose Keep changes or Discard. Open a slate

  1. Send First upload → Preview 10 rows → Import clients.
  2. Send Add Review duplicates after Preview 10 rows.
  3. Send At Review duplicates, Keep the original customer record to give the branch an outcome. Reopen the slate to see it saved. Undo at the canvas's corner, or Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on a Mac), takes back your last change to the map, and leaves a teammate's later changes as they are.
  4. Choose Present to walk a teammate through it.

You can also ask Canvas AI for changes in your own words, with Pro credits on Slatebox Cloud, or your own AI connection on a self-hosted Slatebox. If someone edits while you review a proposal, discard it and ask again.

To add artwork, send “For Review duplicates, set image https://example.org/review.jpg with description A highlighted duplicate customer record” with your own HTTPS image. Try a map with artwork

Start a blank workshop

Example: a field-recording team has one recorder on Friday.

  1. Choose Create slate and send “Make a workshop with Pitches and Record on Friday, limit Record on Friday to one card.” Check the preview and choose Keep changes. The workshop shares the slate's private URL. Create a slate
  2. Send Add card Dawn chorus to Pitches. Add “Ferry horn” and “Rain on glass” the same way.
  3. Drag “Dawn chorus” to Record on Friday, or send Move Dawn chorus to Record on Friday. The lane holds one card, so move it back to Pitches before choosing “Ferry horn”.
To Send
Change a lane's limit Set capacity of Record on Friday to 2
Restrict a card to some lanes Allow Dawn chorus in Pitches, Record on Friday. New lanes stay closed to it until you change the rule.

Give a teammate an edit link to move cards together.

Turn a workshop into a decision

Give a move a consequence. In the theatre example, send Set outcome Entrance call for Warm sidelight up in Door opens: Bring up the sidelight | Hold the actor in shadow, keep it, and move the cue to hear the new call in the presentation. Try the theatre cue

For a budget, try moving $200 of the $600 pilot from a newsletter placement to concierge onboarding and watch assisted imports change. Open it as a slate to keep and share it. Try the pilot budget

Work with a team

To Do this
Give a teammate access Share → Private, then edit or read beside their name.
Invite a new team member A team administrator opens Manage team in the menu.
Share an edit link Share → Create edit link → Copy link → Done. Anyone with the link can edit.
Share a read-only link Share → Unlisted, leave Link permission on read, then Create link.
Change an existing link Choose a permission, then Apply access.

On an interactive workshop, a first-time visitor chooses Join to edit to move a card as a guest. Both of you see the change, and it is still there after a reload. Try a two-person incident handoff

Return to a shared slate

Open My Slates → Recently joined and choose the board. A board appears there after you save an edit through an invitation while signed in, including as a guest. Opening a board only to read it does not add it. Open My Slates

  • Signed out: your edits still save. After the first one, choose Keep in My Slates, then Open My Slates. Otherwise keep the invitation link.
  • Interactive workshop: Join to edit creates a guest session and adds the workshop to your list. Before signing in on another device, open Profile → Save my Slatebox in the same browser.
  • Owner changes the link: switched to read, the link opens the board for viewing. Share → Private → Make private revokes it for good; ask for a new invitation.

Present or publish

Present a whiteboard together

The customer-file sample follows an upload through a fictional import service. Replace its retention note with your own policy before showing a customer. Open the customer-file whiteboard

  1. Choose Share and give your teammate a read link.
  2. Choose Presentation options → Present to everyone here (the arrow beside Present). Your teammate chooses Follow presentation.
  3. Use Next and Previous to move through the notes together.
  4. Your teammate can choose Leave presentation to explore alone. End presentation brings everyone back to the board.

To rehearse alone, choose Present. Try the three views

Present a workshop together

  1. In the conversation, set the order: Present Repair missing rows first, or Present Approve cutover after Compare row counts, using your scene names. Check the sequence and choose Keep changes. Try the backfill review
  2. Choose Share and give your reviewer a read link.
  3. Choose Presentation options → Present to everyone here. The reviewer opens the slate and chooses Follow presentation.
  4. Use Next and Previous. Leave presentation returns the reviewer to the board; End presentation ends the session.

To rehearse alone, choose Present.

Send a presentation for later

Choose Publish presentation on a saved workshop. Readers step through its scenes on their own, in the standalone player or embedded in a handbook. Publish again to include later changes. Publish and embed a walkthrough

Questions

Is Slatebox free?

Yes. Slatebox Cloud is free for individuals and companies alike: unlimited slates, live editing, guests without an account, smart components, presenting, publishing, the API and the MCP server. AI drafts and calls you start come with Slatebox Pro, or from a balance. See pricing

Is Slatebox open source?

Yes. The app, the SlateboxJS engine and the relay are MIT licensed, except the app's enterprise/ folder of outside-agent connections. Licenses

Do teammates need an account to edit with me?

No. Anyone with an edit link can edit; a guest joins without an account. Register before you rely on a slate from another device.

Can my agent draw on my slates?

Yes. Add https://app.slatebox.com/mcp to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any MCP client and sign in. Use Slatebox from your agent
