Slatebox user guide

Read Markdown
View agent prompt
Help me complete my task in Slatebox using the official app guide. Ask which outcome I want, then walk me through the relevant steps and verify it on my slate.

Read https://docs.slatebox.com/Using-Slatebox/readme.md first. Ask for the inputs you need. Tell me what you verified and which steps I still need to do.

If your client cannot fetch the documentation, read the same published file at https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Slatebox/slatebox-docs/retype/Using-Slatebox/readme.md.

Start your first slate

Choose Create slate. Sketch on the canvas or describe the work in the conversation beside it. Try “Draw a flow from Request to Review to Approve” for three connected, editable notes. Paste steps or interview notes to give the conversation material to work with. Create a slate

On Pro or a paid Team plan, ask a follow-up about the drawing. For example, “Diagram the services in these notes. Show which depend on storage, and leave the reporting job separate.” Slatebox can use the notes and connections already on the canvas. Review the proposed notes and arrows, then choose Keep changes to add them.

To see a source and its diagram together, open the call-status example. The two paths show what happened to new and existing calls during an outage. Open the call diagram in a private slate gives you the same drawing to edit. See the call diagram

Select a shape and tap one of its round connection handles. A connected shape appears beside it, ready for you to type its name. To place the new shape somewhere specific, drag the handle into open space. To rename any shape, select it and start typing, or double-tap it. Try a connector on the sound map

Give your slate a recognizable name. If you began as a guest, register the guest account in that browser before relying on access from another device. Keep the canvas link. People opening a private link still need permission.

If a teammate sends you an older template bookmark, the preview shows the template they chose. Choose Use this template to make a private copy. When the link includes a topic, you can see the name your copy will have before you open it.

Add a branch to an interactive map

Ask the side conversation to make the flow interactive. A map has stations, paths between them and a token the team can move. The conversation can name a station, add a branch and change what happens on arrival. Pro and paid Team accounts can also ask Canvas AI to draft changes in their own words. Open a slate

  1. Send First upload → Preview 10 rows → Import clients. Try the path in the preview, then choose Keep changes.
  2. Send Add Review duplicates after Preview 10 rows. Try the new branch. Choose Keep changes to save it or Discard to return to the saved map.
  3. Send At Review duplicates, Keep the original customer record to give the branch an outcome. Keep it and reopen the slate to see the result. Undo last change restores the previous saved map while an undo is available.
  4. Choose Present to walk a teammate through the map. Give a teammate edit access to work on the same slate or read access to show the saved result. If someone edits while you are reviewing a proposal, discard it and ask again on the updated map.

To add artwork, send “For Review duplicates, set image https://example.org/review.jpg with description A highlighted duplicate customer record”, substituting your own HTTPS image and description. Review the change and choose Keep changes. Try a map with artwork

Start a blank workshop

A field-recording team has one recorder on Friday. Open Create slate, then send “Make a workshop with Pitches and Record on Friday, limit Record on Friday to one card.” Check the names and capacity in the preview, then choose Keep changes. The workshop and your original drawing share one private URL. Create a slate

Send each change in the conversation beside the board. Check the preview and choose Keep changes when it looks right.

  1. If you want a different limit later, send Set capacity of Record on Friday to 2. Keep the single slot for this example.
  2. Send Add card Dawn chorus to Pitches. Add “Ferry horn” and “Rain on glass” the same way.
  3. Give a teammate an edit link. Drag “Dawn chorus” to Record on Friday or send Move Dawn chorus to Record on Friday. To choose “Ferry horn” instead, move the chorus back to Pitches first. Share an edit link

To restrict a card to these two destinations, send Allow Dawn chorus in Pitches, Record on Friday. Future lanes stay outside its allowed destinations until you change that rule.

Turn a workshop into a decision

Try the $600 pilot budget. Move a $200 allocation from a newsletter placement to concierge onboarding and watch the number of assisted imports change. Open the budget as a slate to keep that allocation and share it with your cofounder. Try the pilot budget

Give the move a consequence in the conversation. In the theatre example, send Set outcome Entrance call for Warm sidelight up in Door opens: Bring up the sidelight | Hold the actor in shadow. Keep the change and move the cue to hear the new call in the presentation. Try the theatre cue

Work with a team

For a team slate, open Share → Private. Choose edit beside the teammate who will contribute, or read for someone reviewing the board. A team administrator can invite a new member through Manage Team. Manage team members

To invite someone to edit a private slate, open Share, choose Create edit link, then Copy link and Done. Send that unlisted link to your collaborator. Anyone with it can edit. On a regular canvas, they can acknowledge the welcome and edit a note. For an interactive workshop, a first-time visitor can choose Join to edit and make a move as a guest. You should both see the change and find it on the same board after reloading. For a read-only invitation, choose Unlisted, leave Link permission on read and select Create link. To change an existing link, choose a permission and select Apply access. Try a two-person incident handoff

Return to a shared slate

Open My Slates → Recently joined and choose the board’s current title. On a whiteboard, saving an edit through a teammate’s invitation adds that exact link to your list if you are signed in, including as a Slatebox guest. Opening a board to read it leaves your list unchanged.

If you edit while signed out, your changes save to the shared board. After the first saved edit, choose Keep in My Slates to remember this invitation in this browser without registering. When you see Saved to My Slates, choose Open My Slates, then select the board under Recently joined. If you skip this step, keep the invitation to return.

On an interactive workshop, choosing Join to edit creates a guest session and adds the workshop to your list. If you started as a guest, open Profile → Save my Slatebox in the same browser before signing in on another device. Open My Slates

If the owner switches the invitation to read, the same link opens the board for viewing. Opening Share → Private and choosing Make private revokes the invitation and removes the entry. If the owner shares the slate again, ask for the new invitation. The old link stays revoked.

Present or publish

Present a whiteboard together

When a customer asks where their uploaded file goes, show them the path on your whiteboard. The sample follows a file through a fictional import service. Replace its retention note with your own policy before showing it to a customer. Open the customer-file whiteboard

  1. Choose Share and give your teammate a link with read access. Have them open the link while you stay on the board.
  2. Choose Present notes together. Your teammate can choose Follow presentation to see the note you’re discussing.
  3. Use Next to go from the upload to the worker and then to the retained data. Previous takes both of you back when someone has a question.
  4. Your teammate can choose Leave presentation to explore on their own. Choose End presentation to bring everyone still following back to the board.

For a solo rehearsal, choose Present. Try the three views on the product page

Present a workshop together

When a reviewer needs the recovery plan first, open the conversation beside your map and type Present Repair missing rows first, using the scene name on your board. Check the numbered sequence, then choose Keep changes. To move a later scene, type Present Approve cutover after Compare row counts. Try the backfill review

  1. Choose Share and give your reviewer a link with read access.
  2. Choose Present together. The reviewer opens the shared slate and chooses Follow presentation.
  3. Use Next station and Previous to walk through the decision together. The reviewer follows your scene and its arrival instruction. Leave presentation returns the reviewer to the board. End presentation ends the live session.

This also works on saved workshops with lanes and cards. Choose Present for a self-paced rehearsal.

Send a presentation for later

Choose Publish presentation on a saved workshop to share its current scenes with readers. Readers can advance on their own in the standalone player or a team handbook. To include later changes, republish the workshop. Publish and embed a walkthrough

Choose where to work

Individuals can create unlimited slates on the free hosted plan. Pro adds AI capabilities. Team plans support a shared organization. Open the plan chooser for current features and billing options. Organizations with their own infrastructure can operate the app, relay and database together. Hosted plans · Self-host with Docker

Manage a hosted subscription

To change an individual monthly Pro subscription, sign in and open Plans → Annually. If your Pro card offers Switch to annual billing, choose it to review the change in Stripe. Check the annual renewal price and the amount due today. Stripe can credit unused time from your monthly plan. You can leave the review and keep monthly billing, or confirm to switch your existing subscription. On return, the Pro card under Annually shows Current plan · billed annually once Stripe’s update arrives. If the card offers Manage billing, use that to see the options for your subscription. Open hosted plans

Open Profile → Billing to see your subscription in Stripe. If you schedule a cancellation, the portal shows the date your subscription ends. You can keep using paid workshop tools until then. When a Pro subscription ends, your individual account returns to Free. Open your profile
