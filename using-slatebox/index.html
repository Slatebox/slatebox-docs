Slatebox user guide

Start your first slate

Choose Open a blank slate for a freeform canvas. Add a note for the decision, add one for the evidence, and connect them. Use Build an interactive map when participants should move an item along specific paths. Start a slate

To add a thought beside an existing one, select its shape and drag a connection handle into open space. The new shape starts blank and connected. Select it and press F2 to give it a name. Try a connector on the sound map

Give your slate a recognizable name. If you began as a guest, register the guest account in that browser before relying on access from another device. Keep the canvas link. People opening a private link still need permission.

Turn a workshop into a decision

Try the listening room. Move the horn toward the glass chimes until both recordings overlap. Open your mix as a slate, rename a sound source, and present the mix you chose. Your teammate can reopen the same board and try another balance. Try the listening room

Work with a team

For a team slate, open Share → Private. Choose edit beside the teammate who will contribute, or read for someone reviewing the board. A team administrator can invite a new member through team settings. Manage team members

To collaborate by link, open Share → Unlisted, set access to edit, copy the link shown there and choose Save. Send that link to your collaborator. On a regular canvas, they can acknowledge the welcome and edit a note. For an interactive workshop, a first-time visitor can choose Join to edit and make a move as a guest. You should both see the change and find it on the same board after reloading. Choose read for observers or switch back to Private to revoke the unlisted link. Try a two-person incident handoff

Present or publish

Choose Present to lead a meeting from the board. For teammates who will catch up later, publish a sequence of viewpoints. They can open the viewer link or follow the walkthrough inside your team handbook. Publish and embed a walkthrough

Choose where to work

Individuals can create unlimited slates on the free hosted plan. Pro adds AI capabilities. Team plans support a shared organization. Open the plan chooser for current features and billing options. Organizations with their own infrastructure can operate the app, relay and database together. Hosted plans · Self-host with Docker
