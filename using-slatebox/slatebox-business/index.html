Slatebox Business

Read Markdown
View agent prompt
Help me complete my task in Slatebox using the official app guide. Ask which outcome I want, then walk me through the relevant steps and verify it on my slate.

Read https://docs.slatebox.com/Using-Slatebox/slatebox-business.md first. Ask for the inputs you need. Tell me what you verified and which steps I still need to do.

If your client cannot fetch the documentation, read the same published file at https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Slatebox/slatebox-docs/retype/Using-Slatebox/slatebox-business.md.

Slatebox Business is for a team on Slatebox Cloud, the same shared, fully managed service everyone uses. It costs $6 a person a month or $60 a person a year, plus tax where applicable. Every feature but inviting outside agents stays free for every team without it; Business pays for the team's AI and calls in one place. Every member can also invite outside agents onto slates, as with Slatebox Pro. Invite an outside agent Talk to us about Business

What it includes

  • A shared pool of AI and calls for the team. Each member adds the same amount as Pro credits to the pool each month: up to 30 drafts a day for each person, and about $3.75 of AI and calls at what the AI provider and Daily charge us. Anyone on the team draws on it. Slatebox Pro
  • Inviting outside agents, for every member. Business counts as Slatebox Pro for this: anyone on the team can invite an outside agent, such as Excalidraw, Linear or GitHub, onto a slate they can edit. Invite an outside agent
  • Single sign-on and the audit log, free when they come. They are not on Slatebox Cloud yet. When they come to Cloud, they will be free for every team, with or without Business.
  • Hands-on help for large teams.
  • Moving in from a Slatebox you run yourself is coming: slates, files, accounts and sign-in settings, in one step.

AI and calls

  • No own keys on Cloud. AI and calls come from the pool, then a balance. Own AI and call keys are for self-hosted Slatebox. Use your own keys
  • When the pool runs out, a team balance a team admin tops up can take over, at what the provider charges us plus 5%, the same as any top-up. Top-ups and refunds
  • Calls and AI are counted as in Pricing.

How to start

Business is not on sale in the app yet. Until it is, write to us and we set your team up by hand. Talk to us about Business

Run it yourself instead

A Slatebox you run on your own server is free, with no meters and every feature except outside-agent connections, which need an on-prem license there. Run your own Slatebox
