Top-ups and refunds

Read Markdown
View agent prompt
Help me complete my task in Slatebox using the official app guide. Ask which outcome I want, then walk me through the relevant steps and verify it on my slate.

Read https://docs.slatebox.com/Using-Slatebox/top-ups.md first. Ask for the inputs you need. Tell me what you verified and which steps I still need to do.

If your client cannot fetch the documentation, read the same published file at https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Slatebox/slatebox-docs/retype/Using-Slatebox/top-ups.md.

A balance pays for AI and calls once your Pro credits are used, or on its own without Slatebox Pro. It is spent at what the AI provider or Daily charges us, plus a small margin. You do not need Pro to top up. See pricing

Top up

Open your profile, choose Credits, then Top up. Before you pay, the dialog shows the whole amount: the balance you add, and the card fee on its own line. The card fee is what the card network charges; it is not part of the balance.
Amounts The dialog shows the smallest top-up and the largest balance. A first top-up has a lower limit.
Expiry A balance never expires.
Video Video paid from a top-up can start 24 hours after that top-up.

The margin covers the card fee kept on refunds, the fee for a disputed payment, and the gap between token-based prices and the provider's bill. It is not a profit line.

How a balance is spent

Your Pro credits are used first if you have Slatebox Pro, then your balance. A team admin can top up a shared team balance in Team settings; it is used after your own balance.

When the balance runs out, paid use stops. The last request finishes, and there is no bill afterwards.

Refunds

Choose Refund my balance in Credits. What is left goes back to the card it came from; card fees are not refunded.

  • A refund waits while a call or a request is being paid from it. Try again when it ends.
  • A team admin refunds a team balance in Team settings.
  • Deleting your account refunds your balance first.
  • A balance is on hold while its payment is disputed with your bank. Write to us about a held balance at https://slatebox.com/contact/?topic=billing.

Slatebox Pro is not a balance: its fees follow their own rules. Slatebox Pro
