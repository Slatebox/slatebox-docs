Pricing and free amounts

Read Markdown
View agent prompt

Read https://docs.slatebox.com/Using-Slatebox/pricing.md first. Ask for the inputs you need. Tell me what you verified and which steps I still need to do.

If your client cannot fetch the documentation, read the same published file at https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Slatebox/slatebox-docs/retype/Using-Slatebox/pricing.md.

Slatebox is free: unlimited slates, live editing, guests, teams of any size, comments, presenting, publishing, the API, the CLI and the MCP server. No feature or seat subscriptions. Ever. Only AI and calls are metered, because each one costs us money. Every signed-in person gets a free amount of both each month. See pricing

What is free

Free for every signed-in person After that
AI drafting 30 drafts a day Your own AI, or top up
Voice calls you start About 10 hours of four-person calls a month Your own Daily key, or top up
Video calls you start About 2 hours of four-person video a month Your own Daily key, or top up
  • You need an account with a confirmed email address. Guests can edit, comment, present, follow and join calls, but cannot start AI or a call.
  • Free this month, in your profile, shows drafts left today and call hours left this month.
  • The daily count resets at 00:00 UTC. The monthly amounts reset on the 1st (UTC).

How we count

  • A draft is one request: each message AI answers. Typed steps with arrows, pasted lines and named lanes are drawn without AI and cost nothing.
  • Images: Image search and image generation use the month's free AI, not the daily count. An image search costs very little; about 33 generated images use a month's free AI.
  • The month's free AI has a cost limit. A very large request uses more of it. When it runs out, the profile says so, even with drafts left today.
  • Calls are counted in participant-minutes: people on the call times minutes. You get 2,400 participant-minutes of voice and 500 participant-minutes of video a month. A two-person call lasts twice as long as a four-person one.
  • When free video runs out, the call carries on as voice on the same slate. Nobody rejoins.
  • Slatebox asks before a camera turns on, because a camera makes the whole call a video call.
  • A daily limit stops one account using a month in an afternoon. It never cuts a running call short.

Who pays

  • AI: the person who asks. Their own AI, or their team's, costs nothing on Slatebox. Otherwise their free amount is used, then their balance.
  • Calls: the person who starts the call. Joining a call uses nothing of yours. Guests never pay and need no account.
  • Everyone else on the slate pays nothing.

Three ways to pay for AI and calls

  1. Your own key. For AI, a cloud provider with your key or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, chosen per feature. For calls, a Daily key. We charge nothing and set no limit. Choose your AI · Use your own keys
  2. The free amount above, every month.
  3. A balance you top up, from $10, at what the AI provider or Daily charges us plus 5%. The card fee shows as its own line before you pay. Top-ups and refunds

We never charge a card you did not top up. At $0, paid use stops; there is no bill afterwards.

Limits on calls from your free amount or balance

Such a call holds up to 20 people and runs up to 3 hours. It ends when one person has been alone for 10 minutes, or when the free amount or balance paying for it runs out; everyone sees a notice 2 minutes before either. A call on your own Daily key has Daily's limits and none of ours.

The monthly budget

The free amounts come from a shared budget of $1,000 a month. If free use runs high, the free amounts change until the 1st of the next month:

Free use this month What changes
70% of the budget New free video calls start as voice. Running calls carry on.
90% Free AI drops from 30 to 10 drafts a day.
100% New free calls pause, and running free calls end within 10 minutes. AI stays at 10 drafts a day.
110% Free AI pauses.

The 100% rules also apply for the rest of any day (UTC) in which free use reaches one twentieth of the monthly budget. Your own keys and your balance work at every stage. We announce changes to the free amounts 30 days ahead.
