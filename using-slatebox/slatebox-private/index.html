Slatebox Private is a Slatebox for one company, on its own server, run by us. It has every feature, unlimited accounts and unlimited guests. You pay once a year, by size. The waitlist is open. Join the Private waitlist

  • People at once is guidance, not a limit. Storage is the only hard limit. When a server runs hot, we suggest the next size at renewal; nobody is locked out.
  • 15,000 participant-minutes is about 15 people on calls 4 hours a week. Participant-minutes are counted as in Pricing and free amounts.
  • AI is counted in dollars of what the provider charges us, not in drafts: about $100 a month on Small and $400 on Medium. That is about $1 a person at the top of each size's team range, never less than the free Cloud amount.

What every size includes

From the day Private opens, every size includes:

  • Every feature, the same as Slatebox Cloud and a self-hosted Slatebox.
  • Sign-in through your own identity provider.
  • Hosting in the EU or the US.
  • Nightly backups, kept off-site for 30 days.
  • A status page and a 99.5% uptime target.

AI and calls

  • Included AI runs on one provider we set up for you.
  • Your own keys are free. Bring your own AI key or call account and we charge nothing for that part. Use your own keys
  • The included amounts are your company's own. They are paid for in the yearly price, not drawn from the shared budget behind the free Cloud amounts, so they never shrink or pause when that budget runs short.
  • Need more? A usage pack adds 20,000 participant-minutes and about $100 of AI a month for $2,400 a year, on the same invoice. Or top up at our cost plus 5%, the same as Cloud top-ups, up to a monthly ceiling your admin sets.
  • Alerts go to your admin at 50%, 80% and 100% of the month's amount. A running call is never cut off. Once the ceiling is reached, new calls wait.

Paying

  • Billed yearly. 10% off when you pay two years up front, and it renews as another two years at the same 10% off.
  • Tax is added where it applies.
  • A full refund within 30 days of go-live, less any extra usage already spent.
  • No feature or seat subscriptions. Ever. The yearly price pays for hosting; the code and the features are the same everywhere.

Move in and leave

  • Move in: when Private opens, a self-hosted Slatebox moves across in one click from its Admin page. Your slates, files, accounts, passwords and sign-in settings come with it.
  • Leave any time with everything, in one command, from the day Private opens: slates, files, accounts, passwords and sign-in settings, back on your own server, at no cost. Run your own Slatebox
  • If Slatebox LLC stops, you get a working self-hosted copy and 90 days' notice.
