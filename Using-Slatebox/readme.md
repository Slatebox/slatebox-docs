# Slatebox user guide

## Start your first slate

Choose **Open a blank slate** for a freeform canvas. Add a note for the decision, add one for the evidence, and connect them. Use **Build an interactive map** when participants should move an item along specific paths. [Start a slate](https://app.slatebox.com/start)

To add a thought beside an existing one, select its shape and drag a connection handle into open space. The new shape starts blank and connected. Select it and press **F2** to give it a name.

Give your slate a recognizable name. If you began as a guest, register the guest account in that browser before relying on access from another device. Keep the canvas link. People opening a private link still need permission.

## Turn a workshop into a decision

Try the listening room. Move the horn toward the glass chimes until both recordings overlap. Open your mix as a slate, rename a sound source, and present the mix you chose. Your teammate can reopen the same board and try another balance. [Try the listening room](https://slatebox.com/product/workshops/)

## Work with a team

For a team slate, open **Share → Private**. Choose **edit** beside the teammate who will contribute, or **read** for someone reviewing the board. A team administrator can invite a new member through team settings. [Manage team members](../Developers/SlateboxAPI/organizations.md)

To collaborate by link, open **Share → Unlisted**, set access to **edit**, copy the link shown there and choose **Save**. Send that link to your collaborator. On a regular canvas, they can acknowledge the welcome and edit a note. For an interactive workshop, a first-time visitor can choose **Join to edit** and make a move as a guest. You should both see the change and find it on the same board after reloading. Choose **read** for observers or switch back to **Private** to revoke the unlisted link. [Try a two-person incident handoff](../Examples/incident-handoff.md)

## Present or publish

Present your board when the group is ready to walk through a decision. For a public snapshot that can appear on another site, publish a presentation and use its published ID in an iframe. Sharing a snapshot and granting edit access serve different audiences. [Embed a presentation](../Developers/SlateboxAPI/readme.md#embed-a-published-board)

## Choose where to work

Individuals can create unlimited slates on the free hosted plan. Pro adds AI capabilities. Team plans support a shared organization. Open the plan chooser for current features and billing options. Organizations with their own infrastructure can operate the app, relay and database together. [Hosted plans](https://app.slatebox.com/plans) · [Self-host with Docker](../Installation/install-with-docker.md)
