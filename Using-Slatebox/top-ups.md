# Top-ups and refunds

A balance pays for AI and calls once your free amount is used. It is spent at what the AI provider or Daily charges us, plus 5%.

## Top up

Open your profile, choose **Free this month**, then **Top up**. Before you pay, the dialog shows the whole amount, for example: "You pay $10.61: $10.00 of balance and a $0.61 card fee." The card fee is what the card network charges; it is not part of the balance.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Smallest top-up | $10 |
| First top-up | Up to $25 |
| Largest balance | $500 |
| Expiry | A balance never expires. |
| Video | Video paid from a top-up can start 24 hours after that top-up. |

The 5% covers the card fee kept on refunds, $15 for a disputed payment, and the gap between token-based prices and the provider's bill. It is not a profit line.

## How a balance is spent

Your own key is used first, then your free amount, then your balance. A team admin can top up a shared team balance in **Team settings**; it is used after your own balance.

At $0, paid use stops. The last request finishes, and there is no bill afterwards.

## Refunds

Choose **Refund my balance** in **Free this month**. What is left goes back to the card it came from; card fees are not refunded.

- A refund waits while a call or a request is being paid from it. Try again when it ends.
- A team admin refunds a team balance in **Team settings**.
- Deleting your account refunds your balance first.
- A balance is on hold while its payment is disputed with your bank. Write to help@slatebox.com about a held balance.
