# Top-ups and refunds

A balance pays for AI and calls after your free amount is used. It is spent at what the AI provider or Daily charges us, plus 5%.

## Top up

Open your profile, choose **Free this month** and then **Top up**. Before you pay, the dialog shows the whole amount in two lines, for example: "You pay $10.61: $10.00 of balance and a $0.61 card fee." The card fee is what the card network charges for the payment, and is not part of the balance.

- The smallest top-up is $10.
- Your first top-up can be up to $25.
- A balance can hold up to $500.
- A balance never expires.
- Video paid from a top-up can start 24 hours after that top-up.

## Why plus 5%

When we refund a balance, the card network keeps its fee. A disputed payment costs $15. The price of a draft is worked out from the tokens it used, and Daily's bill can differ from its list price. The 5% covers these; it is not a profit line.

## How a balance is spent

Your own key is used first, then your free amount, then your balance. A team can share one balance: a team admin tops it up in **Team settings**, and it is used after your own balance.

When a balance reaches $0, paid use stops. The last request finishes, and there is no bill afterwards.

## Refunds

Choose **Refund my balance** in **Free this month** and we refund what is left to the card it came from. Card fees are not refunded. A refund waits while a call or a request is being paid from it: try again when it ends. A team admin refunds a team balance in **Team settings**. Deleting your account refunds your balance first.

If a payment is disputed with your bank, the balance is put on hold until the dispute is settled. Write to help@slatebox.com about a balance on hold.

## Former subscribers

If you paid a subscription before Slatebox became free, the unused part of your last payment became balance worth 1.5 times that amount. Only the original amount can be refunded to your card; the extra half is for use in Slatebox. The part you can refund goes down as you spend, in proportion: on a $15 balance from a $10 payment, after $6 spent, $6 can be refunded. You also get about 6 hours of four-person video a month (1,500 participant-minutes) for 12 months. Your subscription ends, and it is not charged again.
