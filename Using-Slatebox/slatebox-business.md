# Slatebox Business

Slatebox Business is for a team on Slatebox Cloud, the same shared, fully managed service everyone uses. It costs $6 a person a month or $60 a person a year, plus tax where applicable. Every feature stays free for every team without it; Business pays for the team's AI and calls in one place. [Talk to us about Business](https://slatebox.com/contact/?topic=business)

## What it includes

- **A shared pool of AI and calls for the team.** Each member adds the same amount as Pro credits to the pool each month: 30 drafts a day for each person, and about $4.00 of AI and calls at what the AI provider and Daily charge us. Anyone on the team draws on it. [Slatebox Pro](slatebox-pro.md)
- **Enterprise features, free for every team on Cloud anyway.** Single sign-on and the audit log are not sold separately, with or without Business.
- **Hands-on help for large teams.**
- **Moving in from a Slatebox you run yourself** is coming: slates, files, accounts and sign-in settings, in one step.

## AI and calls

- **Your own keys stay free.** Bring your own AI key or Daily key and we charge nothing for that part. [Use your own keys](own-keys.md)
- **When the pool runs out,** a team balance a team admin tops up can take over, at what the provider charges us plus 5%, the same as any top-up. [Top-ups and refunds](top-ups.md)
- Calls and AI are counted as in [Pricing](pricing.md#how-we-count).

## How to start

Business is not on sale in the app yet. Until it is, write to us through the contact form and we set your team up by hand. [Talk to us about Business](https://slatebox.com/contact/?topic=business)
