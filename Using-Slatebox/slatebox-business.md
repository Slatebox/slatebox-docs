# Slatebox Business

Slatebox Business is for a team on Slatebox Cloud, the same shared, fully managed service everyone uses. It costs $6 a person a month or $60 a person a year, plus tax where applicable. Every feature but inviting outside agents stays free for every team without it; Business pays for the team's AI and calls in one place. Every member can also invite outside agents onto slates, as with Slatebox Pro. [Invite an outside agent](invite-agents.md) [Talk to us about Business](https://slatebox.com/contact/?topic=business)

## What it includes

- **A shared pool of AI and calls for the team.** Each member adds the same amount as Pro credits to the pool each month: up to 30 drafts a day for each person, and about $3.75 of AI and calls at what the AI provider and Daily charge us. Anyone on the team draws on it. [Slatebox Pro](slatebox-pro.md)
- **Inviting outside agents, for every member.** Business counts as Slatebox Pro for this: anyone on the team can invite an outside agent, such as Excalidraw, Linear or GitHub, onto a slate they can edit. [Invite an outside agent](invite-agents.md)
- **Single sign-on and the audit log, free when they come.** They are not on Slatebox Cloud yet. When they come to Cloud, they will be free for every team, with or without Business.
- **Hands-on help for large teams.**
- **Moving in from a Slatebox you run yourself** is coming: slates, files, accounts and sign-in settings, in one step.

## AI and calls

- **No own keys on Cloud.** AI and calls come from the pool, then a balance. Own AI and call keys are for self-hosted Slatebox. [Use your own keys](own-keys.md)
- **When the pool runs out,** a team balance a team admin tops up can take over, at what the provider charges us plus 5%, the same as any top-up. [Top-ups and refunds](top-ups.md)
- Calls and AI are counted as in [Pricing](pricing.md#how-we-count).

## How to start

Business is not on sale in the app yet. Until it is, write to us and we set your team up by hand. [Talk to us about Business](https://slatebox.com/contact/?topic=business)
