# Slatebox Private

Slatebox Private is a Slatebox for one company, on its own server, run by us. It has every feature, unlimited accounts and unlimited guests. You pay once a year, by size. ## What every size includes

Every size includes:

- Every feature, the same as Slatebox Cloud and a self-hosted Slatebox.
- Sign-in through your own identity provider.<!-- feature:self-host --> [Single sign-on](../Installation/single-sign-on.md)<!-- /feature:self-host -->
- Hosting in the EU or the US.
- Nightly backups, kept off-site for 30 days.
- A status page and a 99.5% uptime target.

## AI and calls

- **Included AI** runs on one provider we set up for you.
- **Your own keys are free.** Bring your own AI key or call account and we charge nothing for that part. [Use your own keys](own-keys.md)
- **The included amounts are your company's own.** They are paid for in the yearly price, not drawn from anyone's Slatebox Pro or from a shared budget, so they never shrink or pause when Cloud use runs high.
- **Need more?** A usage pack adds 20,000 participant-minutes and about $100 of AI a month for $2,400 a year, on the same invoice. Or top up at our cost plus 5%, the same as Cloud top-ups, up to a monthly ceiling your admin sets.
- **Alerts** go to your admin at 50%, 80% and 100% of the month's amount. A running call is never cut off. Once the ceiling is reached, new calls wait.
